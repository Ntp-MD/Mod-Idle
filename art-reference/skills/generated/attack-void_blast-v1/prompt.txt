Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: production raster VFX animation atlas for a first-party isometric fantasy ARPG.
Primary request: a completely new VOID BLAST skill animation, a hollow ring that blooms, sucks inward, then snaps outward.

Design a distinctive chunky void silhouette, with a violet-white core, violet (#a970d8) mid-tones, indigo edges, and frayed ribbon ends.

Use crisp hand-painted pixel clusters / stepped edges matching a polished chibi ARPG; avoid a flat neon ring with no depth, smooth neon vector rings, photographic void, excessive blur, or black outlines.
Output: a genuinely TRANSPARENT square PNG sprite atlas. Target canvas 2048 by 2048, arranged as EXACTLY 4 columns by 4 rows, 16 equal square cells. The entire image consists of these 16 cells with invisible grid boundaries, no captions, no numbers, no borders, no labels.
Animation order is row-major:
Row 1, cells 0-3: four opening frames, a dark seed spreading into a thin ring. Gradual growth; no sudden teleport.
Row 2, cells 4-7: first four full-strength storm frames.
Row 3, cells 8-11: next four full-strength storm frames. Together cells 4-11 form one seamless 8-frame churning loop. Row 4, cells 12-15: four exit frames, the ring snaps outward into frayed ribbons; the last frame is only faint indigo.
Every cell has exactly the same fixed virtual canvas and ground/contact anchor at 88 percent cell height, horizontally centred. The full-strength form is about 68% of cell width and 64% of cell height. All visible ring, ribbons, shards remain completely INSIDE their own cell, with at least 8 percent clear transparent padding on all four cell edges. The anchor stays fixed throughout opening, loop and exit. No element may touch or cross any cell border. Do not enlarge a frame to fill its cell.
Camera: fixed three-quarter ARPG view, slightly above, looking toward a broad dark ring over its ground circle. Keep the same camera and anatomy in every frame.

This atlas is visual art only; no hit counts, targets, damage numbers, or gameplay symbols.
