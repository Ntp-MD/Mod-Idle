Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: production raster VFX animation atlas for a first-party isometric fantasy ARPG.
Primary request: a completely new EARTH SPLITTER skill animation, a single ground-rent that heaves angular slabs skyward at one point.
This is a heaving rent, not a character and not a skill icon.
Design a distinctive chunky stone silhouette, with a sunlit ochre core, ochre-brown (#a4723f) mid-tones, umber edges, and tumbling grit and dust.
It should feel rending, seismic, massive while remaining readable at small game scale.
Use crisp hand-painted pixel clusters / stepped edges matching a polished chibi ARPG; avoid a wide flat puddle with no heave, smooth neon vector rings, photographic stone, excessive blur, or black outlines.
Output: a genuinely TRANSPARENT square PNG sprite atlas. Target canvas 2048 by 2048, arranged as EXACTLY 4 columns by 4 rows, 16 equal square cells. The entire image consists of these 16 cells with invisible grid boundaries, no captions, no numbers, no borders, no labels.
Animation order is row-major:
Row 1, cells 0-3: four opening frames, a hairline crack splitting open at one ground point. Gradual growth; no sudden teleport.
Row 2, cells 4-7: first four full-strength storm frames.
Row 3, cells 8-11: next four full-strength storm frames. Together cells 4-11 form one seamless 8-frame churning loop. Show actual evolving shapes and churning pressure, slabs grinding higher between frames, stable overall size, not eight identical copies. Frame 11 transitions cleanly back to frame 4.
Row 4, cells 12-15: four exit frames, the slabs crash back into dust and rubble; the last frame is only faint dust.
Every cell has exactly the same fixed virtual canvas and ground/contact anchor at 88 percent cell height, horizontally centred. The full-strength form is about 52% of cell width and 60% of cell height. All visible slabs, rubble, dust remain completely INSIDE their own cell, with at least 8 percent clear transparent padding on all four cell edges. The anchor stays fixed throughout opening, loop and exit. No element may touch or cross any cell border. Do not enlarge a frame to fill its cell.
Camera: fixed three-quarter ARPG view, slightly above, looking toward heaving slabs over a single ground point. Keep the same camera and anatomy in every frame.
Lighting: bright hot core, rich stone surface, restrained soft alpha only at dust edges. No baked black or white backdrop, no checkerboard, no terrain, no ground shadow, no UI, no characters, no weapons, no text. Transparency must be real alpha.
This atlas is visual art only; no hit counts, targets, damage numbers, or gameplay symbols.
