Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: production raster VFX animation atlas for a first-party isometric fantasy ARPG.
Primary request: a completely new ACID ARROW skill animation, a thin glassy arrow that flies true then pits into corrosive drops.
This is an arrow, not a character and not a skill icon.
Design a distinctive chunky venom silhouette, with a lime highlight core, leaf-green (#6dbf57) mid-tones, olive sludge edges, and drip and bubble.
It should feel glassy, boring, corrosive while remaining readable at small game scale.
Use crisp hand-painted pixel clusters / stepped edges matching a polished chibi ARPG; avoid a blunt green blob with no shaft, smooth neon vector rings, photographic venom, excessive blur, or black outlines.
Output: a genuinely TRANSPARENT square PNG sprite atlas. Target canvas 2048 by 2048, arranged as EXACTLY 4 columns by 4 rows, 16 equal square cells. The entire image consists of these 16 cells with invisible grid boundaries, no captions, no numbers, no borders, no labels.
Animation order is row-major:
Row 1, cells 0-3: four opening frames, a glassy sliver condensing into a thin arrow. Gradual growth; no sudden teleport.
Row 2, cells 4-7: first four full-strength flight frames, head leading with tail trailing left.
Row 3, cells 8-11: next four full-strength flight frames. Together cells 4-11 form one seamless 8-frame flight loop. Show actual evolving shapes and straight true flight, shaft steady between frames, stable overall size, not eight identical copies. Frame 11 transitions cleanly back to frame 4.
Row 4, cells 12-15: four exit frames, a pitting burst that eats into drops; the last frame is only faint vapour.
Every cell has exactly the same fixed virtual canvas and head centred in the cell with the tail pointing left; the last four frames read as a single radial burst from the cell centre. The full-strength form is about 32% of cell width and 26% of cell height. All visible shaft, drops, vapour remain completely INSIDE their own cell, with at least 8 percent clear transparent padding on all four cell edges. The anchor stays fixed throughout opening, loop and exit. No element may touch or cross any cell border. Do not enlarge a frame to fill its cell.
Camera: fixed three-quarter ARPG view, slightly above, tracking a thin arrow in side flight with a centred pitting burst. Keep the same camera and anatomy in every frame.
Lighting: bright hot core, rich venom surface, restrained soft alpha only at vapour edges. No baked black or white backdrop, no checkerboard, no terrain, no ground shadow, no UI, no characters, no weapons, no text. Transparency must be real alpha.
This atlas is visual art only; no hit counts, targets, damage numbers, or gameplay symbols.
