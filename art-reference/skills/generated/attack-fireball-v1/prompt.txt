Use case: first-party authored raster VFX, kitbash/composite only; no external image-generation service.
Asset: attack.fireball active-skill projectile and impact, 16-frame 4x4 raster atlas, 352px cells, 1408x1408.
Source material: the approved first-party attack.firestorm v1 atlas (game/public/fx/authored/attack-firestorm/v1/atlas-v1.png). Reuse its fire texture; do not import any third-party pack.
Method: crop the dense upper vortex ring for the fireball head; crop the lower funnel and rotate it for the trailing flame; circular alpha mask plus a soft radial vignette; small white-hot core overlay; procedural embers, flash, smoke and a broken pressure ring are not baked into the atlas.
Animation order is row-major:
Row 1, cells 0-3: ignition. A small ember gathers into a compact fireball; the last frame grows a short flame tail.
Row 2, cells 4-7: flight loop. The head is centred in the cell, the tail trails to the left; four full-strength turbulent frames.
Row 3-4, cells 8-15: impact. A bright flash, an expanding fireball, drifting smoke and embers; the last frame is mostly smoke and a few faint embers.
Every cell has at least 8 percent clear transparent padding on all four edges. The flight head sits at the cell centre (anchor 0.5, 0.5); the impact expands from the centre. Transparency is real alpha; no baked background, checkerboard, terrain, UI, labels, captions, numbers or borders.
Keep the Firestorm fire material and chunky hand-painted ARPG look; do not smooth it into neon vector fire or photographic smoke. This atlas is visual art only; no hit counts, targets, damage numbers or gameplay symbols.
