Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: production raster VFX animation atlas for a first-party isometric fantasy ARPG.
Primary request: a completely new ICE LANCE skill animation, a slender frost lance that draws back, streaks straight, and lands twice before a frost ring closes.
This is a lance, not a character and not a skill icon.
Design a distinctive chunky frost silhouette, with a pale blue-white core, ice-blue (#9fe0f2) mid-tones, deep teal edges, and frost dust and angular shards.
It should feel sharp, piercing, chilling while remaining readable at small game scale.
Use crisp hand-painted pixel clusters / stepped edges matching a polished chibi ARPG; avoid a static icicle with no flight direction, smooth neon vector rings, photographic frost, excessive blur, or black outlines.
Output: a genuinely TRANSPARENT square PNG sprite atlas. Target canvas 2048 by 2048, arranged as EXACTLY 4 columns by 4 rows, 16 equal square cells. The entire image consists of these 16 cells with invisible grid boundaries, no captions, no numbers, no borders, no labels.
Animation order is row-major:
Row 1, cells 0-3: four opening frames, a pale glint gathering into a slender lance head. Gradual growth; no sudden teleport.
Row 2, cells 4-7: first four full-strength flight frames, head leading with tail trailing left.
Row 3, cells 8-11: next four full-strength flight frames. Together cells 4-11 form one seamless 8-frame flight loop. Show actual evolving shapes and straight true flight with shivering frost dust between frames, stable overall size, not eight identical copies. Frame 11 transitions cleanly back to frame 4.
Row 4, cells 12-15: four exit frames, a twin-flash impact that settles into a thin frost ring; the last frame is only faint cold haze.
Every cell has exactly the same fixed virtual canvas and head centred in the cell with the tail pointing left; the last four frames read as a single radial burst from the cell centre. The full-strength form is about 38% of cell width and 28% of cell height. All visible lance, frost dust, impact flash remain completely INSIDE their own cell, with at least 8 percent clear transparent padding on all four cell edges. The anchor stays fixed throughout opening, loop and exit. No element may touch or cross any cell border. Do not enlarge a frame to fill its cell.
Camera: fixed three-quarter ARPG view, slightly above, tracking a slender head-first lance in side flight with a centred ring close. Keep the same camera and anatomy in every frame.
Lighting: bright hot core, rich frost surface, restrained soft alpha only at haze and glow edges. No baked black or white backdrop, no checkerboard, no terrain, no ground shadow, no UI, no characters, no weapons, no text. Transparency must be real alpha.
This atlas is visual art only; no hit counts, targets, damage numbers, or gameplay symbols.
