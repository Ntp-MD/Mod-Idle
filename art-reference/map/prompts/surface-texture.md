# Flat surface texture for a hex mesh

Use when geometry supplies the hex boundary and camera projection. Do not attach the projected hex mask as a framing instruction.

```text
Create one seamless square game terrain texture of [MATERIAL: grass / forest floor / rock / water / snow]. True top-down view, flat material, no perspective or camera tilt. Painterly fantasy tabletop style, muted natural palette matching [STYLE REFERENCE]. Even broad illumination; no directional cast shadows. No hex silhouette, tile platform, border, isolated trees, buildings, characters, roads, text or UI. Material fills the entire image and continues naturally across all four edges. Target resolution [WIDTH] × [HEIGHT].
```

Inspect repeated tiling and texel scale on the actual mesh. A flat texture does not establish the camera view of objects placed above it.
