This first pass reviews thumbnail contact sheets for each species' `animation/atlas/animation.png`, with full-resolution spot checks on low-contrast examples. Grades consider silhouette readability, palette contrast, identity, pose expression, and pixel-art cohesion.

| Grade | Meaning |
| ----- | ------- |
| 5 | Standout and immediately readable |
| 4 | Strong, with minor polish opportunities |
| 3 | Serviceable, with a visible weakness in contrast, detail, or distinctiveness |
| 2 | Weak enough to prioritize for a visual pass |
| 1 | Major readability or rendering problem |

These are species-level overview grades, not frame-by-frame approval. They do not assess exact direction, action-phase accuracy, or atlas readiness.
