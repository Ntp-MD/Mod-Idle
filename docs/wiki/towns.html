Settlements

A settlement is where you spend what the field paid: space, time and information — never power. A price here is written as m, minutes of your own junk income, and charged at the band of the place that sells it.

Where

A settlement adds no clicks to automate and no power. Its stall stock grows with your Standing there, and the Waypoint is opened by walking to it — see Travel.

Who sells what

An NPC vendor never sells gear, Mods or potions — the stalls buy space, time, information and appearance. The one owner-sanctioned exception is the Black Market inside the Market screen, where gold buys rare materials, craft stones and named Skill XP Levels (Economy).

Standing

Earned from kills in that settlement's own zone (F1), plus tasks and boss kills there — both are extra progress on top of these thresholds, never a substitute for them — and Standing is never bought. What it grants: vendor stock lines · Collector set slots · map knowledge · saved filter presets · titles.

The task board

Read through engine.taskPayout, so a task is a slice of the income you would have earned anyway — the board never mints a stone out of nothing. At the 15-minute slice: low band 4 Tier Stones · 1 Add Stone · 13 Reroll Value Stones · mid band 5 Tier Stones · 1 Add Stone · 17 Reroll Value Stones · high band 5 Tier Stones · 1 Add Stone · 22 Reroll Value Stones. A plain kill-count task is gone: the board asks for Elites, named subzone Expeditions and bosses, and pays stones only. Expeditions count only the target Species’ Elites killed while standing on the target subzone; there is no deadline. Claim rewards at a settlement; if the stone bag is full, the task stays claimable until there is room.

The Collector

A piece that improves what you wear stays available for Auto Equip. The Collector reserves non-upgrades before a filter can dissolve them (collector_sets.rule). The Collector pays neither gold nor a Mod — that is what keeps it out of the power economy.
