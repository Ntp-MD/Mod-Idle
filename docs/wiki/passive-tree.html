The passive tree

Every node holds one rank and opens from any owned neighbour; Start is free and every region opens from it. Normal nodes form an irregular field of branching roads and local loops that cross region boundaries, and every playable route ends on a Notable or Keystone. Ordinary dead ends are retired: they keep only their layout position and cannot be bought. Notables sit on local loops or beside roads. Every Keystone has its own Notable entrance from Normal roads. Either branch is open without the other's Notable; earlier roads may join. A build can mix regions from its allocated frontier. Select a node to preview the route; Shift-click buys it when both wallets can pay. A Normal grants exactly +1 of its region's Core attribute for 1 tree point. A Notable grants its value and percent lines for 3 tree points. A Keystone changes a mechanic for 1 attainment point and never spends tree points. treePoints = base 3 + 3 a level from 2–100 + 1 a level from 101–190 + claimed quest bonuses − spent tree. The first milestone Expedition claim per zone and first milestone Delivery completion per town guarantee +1 Tree Point, outside the consumable reward choice. The point pays once on completion; skipping or expiry leaves the milestone available. Legacy saved random Tree Point promises remain payable. Respec at a settlement is free and hands both wallets back at the prices the spec charged. The redesigned roads preserve existing v3 allocations on live nodes, node effects and wallets; a rank on a retired ordinary node is refunded on load, and every live owned node remains a usable frontier. Characters predating v3 are refused with their own message rather than rewritten. The Build goals lens reads what Notables and Keystones grant for staying alive, managing mana, supporting your skills or the held weapon. It quotes the same route and the same tree/attainment split the path purchase charges, marks a route you cannot afford yet and a keystone pair that cannot be held together, and describes a keystone’s trade-off. A recommendation never allocates a point and is not a claim that the build is best. Track this node only pins it in Journey; selection, search, route preview and path purchase stay yours.

Passive points

Progression banks the points; the Build plan spends them.

Levels, quest milestones and first-time Expedition/Delivery milestones bank tree points; a Keystone is paid in attainment, not tree points. Respec is free at a settlement. The tree board is on Passive tree; every rule is on Keystones, and the plan is on Build plan.
