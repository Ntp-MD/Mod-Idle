Crafting

Stones change what a piece is; two of them also move how high it sits — Ascend the band, Upgrade the Gear line. At the settlement Craft bench, right-click a stone and click an equipped or carried item, or a highlighted Mod line for a targeted use. Hold Shift to keep using the stone; Esc or right-click cancels. Every price below is read, never typed.

Use the Craft bench

Gear crafting is a Settlement service. Camp cooking is the field exception; it does not craft gear.

  1. Drag a piece from Equipped or Inventory into the central bench slot.
  2. Hover a gear piece to inspect its full item detail and current Mod lines.
  3. Drag a stone from the stack onto the selected piece. Direct presses apply on drop; presses that need a line or Mod choice prepare the action for review and confirmation.
  4. Hover the gear again after the press to read its updated Mod lines.

The Stones pane opens on Basic, which keeps the common presses together. Switch to Advanced for whole-piece and rare stones: Polish, Reforge, Rebirth, Corrupt, Imprint and the bulk presses.

Forge a new piece

character level ≥ band floor · smithing ≥ 1 / 4 / 10 by grade · cost = pattern count · XP = 60 / 200 / 500 by grade. No gold fee; fancy Base variants stay drop-only.

At a settlement Forge choose a pattern and one material. The material's family picks the Base or weapon Frame; its grade sets the band (low 1–30 · mid 31–60 · high 61–90). The piece rolls with the drop skeleton: line 1, the Bound pair and 2–5 Unbound lines.
