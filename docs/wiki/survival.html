Survival, field skills and provisions

A fight you lose costs time, never a piece and never a level. Read the pools, player-set Field Camp, Hunger, field provisions and the farm that feeds alchemy.

What a Push costs

Nothing on this list is a number: a Push is the cost of the fight, not a tax on the character.

  • There is no death.rulea fight you lose rests you at camp and costs time only
  • A Push on the walk is the ordinary Push.rulewith a Field Camp in this zone, recovery returns there and ends the walk; otherwise the route resumes at its ambush block (road.push_rule)
  • A dungeon run forfeits its escrow.rulethe one place a Push costs loot — World
  • Your preset goes back to the main one.ruleand a cooldown already counting keeps counting (presets.cooldowns_persist_on_push)
  • A broken piece contributes nothing.rulethe Armourer clears it and refills protection — Crafting

Field Camp and life skills

The first Field Camp and one relocation are free; later moves cost 1 ore and 5 timber. It is safe ground in its zone, allows field gathering and cooking, and receives a Push. Only one meal buff is active at a time. Field skill XP and Mastery are separate from character XP.

Hunger and provisions

Walking, hunting and gathering drain Hunger at their own configured rates. Town and a safe idle Camp pause it. Auto Eat defaults to 25%, uses cooked food → fruit → edible herbs, and saves raw food for empty-Hunger emergencies unless opted in earlier. At empty Hunger, HP regeneration is cut by 90%; Energy Shield recharge stays unchanged. Each crossed road block also rolls a separate supply or rest event, in addition to its usual ambush chance.
