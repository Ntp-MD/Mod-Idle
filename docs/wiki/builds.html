Builds, skill pieces and the finish line

Skills are found, not bought, and a skill you already own is climbed by finding the same piece again. What a build is measured against is published, so the target is a number you can check rather than a guide someone wrote.

Finding a skill

A skill piece is its own stream, separate from gear and stones (skill_drop.note). The roster itself is on Skills.

The duplicate ladder

12 duplicates is the whole ladder. A skill’s own level climbs on kills while it is slotted or toggled on, up to 20, and that level is account-wide (presets.skill_xp_is_account_wide) — the two ramps are independent: the ladder buys cooldown, the level buys damage and effect strength.

What an aura holds back

Reserve tiers are skills.json reserve_tiers, and the total may not reach 100% — an aura’s cost is a budget you read up front, not a number you recompute mid-fight. A paid row has one typed flat cost: Mana or current HP. Both climb only with the skill’s own level; pool size controls affordability, not price. HP costs leave one HP, and only Mana costs receive the AoE premium.

Presets

Keys are client settings, not character state — a preset is a loadout, never a key map. A preset activates only owned skills and obeys the same aura reservation limit as the Skills screen.

The finish line

There is no prestige and no reset: the level Cap is 190, Paragon points run from 101, and the tree pays 1 point a level (stat.tree_points_per_level). Remaining goals are counted, never timed — see Crafting and Progression.
