The world

Zones climb in level and in what they pay. A body class is the shape of a fight, not a stat.

Body classes

A boss is not a size. Each boss declares the body class a weapon reads it as; the default is Large. The base evasion is a percent on top of the Species Dex line.

Zones

The band decides the Item quality of what drops there. Each side of a zone fields its own pack, Elite rate and body mix, and a zone’s three sides average back to the group and the Elite rate published above — a crowded side and a heavy side are a choice of ground, not a rate. The Elements column is what a spawn here actually wears — each Species’ own Element, plus the zone Boss’ declared one — read off the cast, so it is never a limit on what can appear.

Dungeons

One dungeon per zone, entered at wild-side index 0. Same mobs, same rolls as the zone — loot waits in escrow until the last mob falls. Retreat rescues 1–2 random legal stacks, forfeits the rest and starts the same 60 minute cooldown as a clear. Normal bag capacity and gear filters still apply; the recap reports selected, received and rejected amounts. Empty Retreat also cools down. A Push forfeits everything. Cooldown pauses offline.
