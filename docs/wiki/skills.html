Skills

Every skill is found as a drop. New buffs and auras enter the collection switched off; turn them on in Skills when you want their effects. A paid row uses one typed flat Mana or current-HP cost; an aura reserves Max Mana. Each owned skill earns XP once per kill while slotted or toggled on; repeating it in the rotation grants no extra XP. A skill levels up to 20 — and at that level a press deals 128.5% of its level-1 damage, a buff's or an aura's effect climbs to 180%, and every other effect reaches 128.5%.

The roster

Min and Max are the same list at the two ends of the skill-level ramp: a press reads its damage, everything else the lines it moves, and each line names itself. The damage figure is a press on the reference character, before crit. Only the arrow rows carry a weapon tag (`bow` = bow or crossbow) and fire solely while one is held; every other row is open to any weapon.

Cost and Max HP terms

Mana pool size changes how many presses are affordable, not their flat price. HP pool size does not change an HP price either; HP costs leave one point.

RuleHow the press reads itData term
Flat costA paid active skill has either a Mana cost or an HP cost, never both; skill level raises the configured flat price.mana · hp_cost
HP safetyHP cost comes from current HP and cannot spend the final point.hp_cost
Caster Max HPA skill may add a share of the caster’s Max HP before the skill-level ramp, crit, weapon and damage-bucket multipliers.max_hp_pct
Target Max HPA separate skill effect may add a share of the target’s Max HP; it is not the caster term.max_hp_pct_damage
Area premiumOnly a Mana-cost press receives the configured AoE cost premium.aoe

What a status changes, and how far it climbs

Every figure is the status table the roll reads. A ROLLED status — an Element status, the bleed an axe’s line grants, or a stun — lands only when the Alignment gate lets it, and 20% of the Element hits that pass then carry their status. A row that declares its status outright is not a roll and never meets the gate.
