Windup, release and impact

A live cast pays its Mana or HP and starts its cooldown at release, not when the windup begins. Total windup is fixed + variable: haste (global_speed, under caps.speed_mult) compresses only the variable part, on separate windup and recovery levers, and neither phase finishes below the combat-step floor or the animation phase floor. Clock modifiers stay separate: cast speed changes variable windup and recovery, cooldown rate moves cooldowns, and attack-interval plus rank clock data govern basic and mob attack opportunities (tools/data/engine.json). Damage and statuses land at impact: melee checks range then, a projectile travels from its release aim and may meet another body, and a ground circle tests living positions at its committed centre. Split Shot keeps its release-committed target circle while the projectile travels — a later contact updates receipts, never the circle. A zero-radius line still holds every body along its committed direction. A chain row reuses its radius as the hop reach between contacts. A canceled windup costs nothing; recovery blocks the next primary cast but never re-prices it. A held target stays selected during tracking and windup; release does not silently acquire a different body. Heals land on the player, never on a mob. A skill hit that crits can fire a crit-trigger aura without taking over the primary cast. Mob projectile and ground casts track from release; a mob charge travels to the point frozen at windup.

How to read a row

Quick tuning: swap delivery to change feel; area.radius_m for circle size; cast.range_m for reach; origin to move the explosion. Never hand-write Hits — set action plus effects: hits / hit_pct / dot_dps+dot_sec and let hitLineOf render it.

ExampleWhat the row saysWhat it means
attack.fireballType=attack · Delivery=projectile · Origin=targetA targeted projectile; the row defines acquisition, cost and impact settings
attack.firestormOrigin=target · Delivery=groundA committed ground circle around an acquired target; the row defines its radius and timing
attack.chain_lightningaction=chain-strikeAoE is the hop reach, not a circle: contacts jump to the nearest unused living bodies using the row target count, share and cadence
attack.piercing_shotshape=lineTarget can read Single Enemy, but the committed line holds every body along its authored length and width

Current combat features (C1–C4)

Hit classes are explicit data. Spell Dodge has no base chance, is tree-only and obeys the cap in tools/data/engine.json; the Wis-wing Spellward node and its Evasion tradeoff are defined in tools/data/tree.json. DoT ticks do not make an incoming-hit roll.

FeatureStatusWhat runs today
Cluster children (C1)Implemented; no live adopterThe data-driven cluster action splits one press across its parent and children; children leave the parent impact on the authored spread and reuse its travel and delay. Overlap can hit the same body with separate impact rolls; kill credit stays once. No skill row uses it yet; Double Strike remains an unrelated melee row.
Chain and reactions (C2)Existing behavior preservedHops continue after a resolved attempt; chain kills pay rewards except Profane Bloom's nonrecursive, unrewarded secondaries.
Lock-on bursts (C3)Implemented and authoredDouble Strafe fires straight payloads along the release aim; Seeker Strafe homes on its bound body. Both use the row's payload count, interval, range and cost.
Spell dodge (C4)Implemented for incoming hitsIncoming attack hits roll Evasion, spell hits roll Spell Dodge, and none skips both. Perfect Dodge and Ghost Dance apply to spells; ignores_dodge bypasses avoidance. Player casts retain the target mob's existing dodge.

Setup → payoff

Payoff rows are Detonate (burn), Shatter (chill), Overload (shock), Catalyst (poison), Ruin (mark) and Aftershock (cripple). A payoff row names its status once, in its own effect.

Presets

Keys are client settings, not character state — a preset is a loadout, never a key map. A preset activates only owned skills and obeys the same aura reservation limit as the Skills screen.
