ModWorld — how to play

An open-world idle RPG. Your character fights while the window is open and while it is shut.

A run, in six steps

The whole game is one screen — the field — and the panels behind it.

  1. Pick a settlement and a zone. In the field, a player-set Camp opens gathering and cooking.
  2. Fight — the character attacks on the clock; paid skills use flat Mana or current-HP costs, while auras reserve Max Mana.
  3. Loot — kills pay XP and their separate gear, junk, stone and skill-piece rolls; hunting can also yield field provisions.
  4. Decide — equip the piece, or feed it to the crafting stones.
  5. Craft at a settlement: choose gear for the bench, right-click a stone, then click the item or a highlighted Mod line. Shift-click repeats; Esc cancels.
  6. Climb to the next zone. A Push costs recovery time: a Field Camp in that zone receives you and ends the walk; otherwise the route resumes at its ambush block.
