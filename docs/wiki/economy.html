Haggling

level = min(cap, floor(sqrt(XP / levelDivisor)) + 1); progress = (level − 1) / (cap − 1).

saleMult = 1 + progress × saleBonusMax / 100; deliveryMult = 1 + progress × deliveryBonusMax / 100; serviceMult = 1 − progress × serviceDiscountMax / 100. A completed Delivery pays XP once.

Regional gathering

harvestBonus = reachedMilestones × bonusPerMilestone; dropChance = min(1, baseChance × (1 + min(cap, (max(0, skill − 1) × skillRate + max(0, mastery − 1) × masteryRate)) / 100)). Regional source selects Mine, Grove and forage goods by zone.

Delivery wants

offer roll = trigger_chance at each online interval for each visited town. The displayed reward and deadline are fixed when offered; each hand-in pays the live market quote, and the completion bonus pays once at the full count.

Deadlines use credited game time and pause during Camp recovery or dungeon runs. Offline time creates no offers. A full bag refuses the final hand-in without spending goods or progress.

Black Market

material price = peakTradeValue × resaleCover × rarityMultiplier. Stock is per town and refreshes online. Skill offers only grant or raise a skill; they never lower level or add duplicate rungs.

What is deliberately absent

  • One sanctioned sink sells power.exceptionthe Black Market sells named Skill XP Levels and craft stones for gold — every other stall still sells space, time, information and appearance
  • No player auction.ruletrade is between the character and town buyers
  • No prestige currency.rulenothing resets what you earned — Builds
  • No timed shop, no sale.ruletime is not a design constraint, so no screen sells urgency
