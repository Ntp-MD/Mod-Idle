Combat

One incoming hit resolves in a fixed order — avoidance, then Armour, then Elemental resistance, then a pool. Every number is the engine at the reference build (every point spent evenly across the seven Core attributes, level 190), so it is the line your own sheet is measured against, not a promise about it.

The order a hit resolves

Hit order: Perfect dodge → Evasion → Block → Armour → resistance → ES → HP. Poison and bleed DoT bypass ES.

Elite, Unique and Boss

BaseHP(L, Core) = mobHpAt(L) + hp_per_level × (L − 1) + Core.VIT × K_VIT_HP. speciesPsMult is profile power ÷ zone-mean species power; profile power = power_base + K_MOB_PS_STAT × (wPhys × STR + wMagic × INT). L is the Species zone level; Unique replaces a normal spawn at zone top level. Boss and Unique have no extra HP multiplier. XP multipliers: Elite ×3 · Boss ×15 · Unique ×30.
