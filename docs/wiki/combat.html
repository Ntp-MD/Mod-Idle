Combat

One incoming hit resolves in a fixed order — avoidance, then Armour, then Elemental resistance, then a pool. Every number is the engine at the reference build (every point spent evenly across the seven Core stats, level 190), so it is the line your own sheet is measured against, not a promise about it.

The order a hit resolves

Hits of every Element, including chaos, and burn DoT spend Energy Shield before Health. Poison and bleed DoT go straight to Health. A status lands on its own roll — the Alignment gate is on Skills.

Elite and Boss

An Elite is a spawn flag forced onto a Large body, never a fifth size, so no two multipliers ever stack — it keeps the selected Variant's name and junk. A Boss is one named creature per zone (18 of them) and is the only place the Add, Replace and Corrupt stones are paid — see Away & bag. A Species may have no Unique or several (35 total): each is a wild boss that replaces its Species' spawn, paying Elite stones with ×30 XP. A mob uses its Variant's authored level band (mob.variants[].levels), which is why the Bestiary prints each band.
