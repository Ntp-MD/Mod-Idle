## License terms (all packs below)

- Commercial use and modification are allowed; attribution is not required.
- Unity's support article confirms Asset Store assets may be used with other engines when the EULA
  is followed: <https://support.unity.com/hc/en-us/articles/34387186019988>
- Not allowed: redistributing the asset standalone, letting end users extract the raw files,
  forum pooling, training AI/ML models.
- `License type: Extension Asset` → seat license, max 2 machines (EULA 2.3.2).
- Full terms: <https://unity.com/legal/as-terms>

## Acquisition status — 2026-11-11

All 21 VFX-content packs in the register were added to the owner's Unity **My Assets** library
through Playwright CLI. Each product page confirmed ownership with "You purchased this item on
Nov 11, 2026." Particle System Color Changer and the ten Projectile Factory adapters were skipped:
they contain no VFX art.

All 21 packages were downloaded through the signed-in Editor's Asset Store download manager.
Original `.unitypackage` files remain in the local Unity Asset Store cache. Extracted source assets
are local-only under `.cache/unity-pack-sources/`.

The local Unity import project is `.cache/vfx-import` (Unity 6000.6.8f1). Selected visual assets were copied into this project;
demo scripts and binaries were excluded. Cartoon FX uses its reviewed shader importer to compile
its original `.cfxrshader` materials for URP. The runtime uses the 15 source packs listed below. KCISA's additional in-package license review remains pending; its art is not shipped.

## Rebuild and review

1. Populate the local import project with the required visual assets, retaining their `.meta` files.
2. Run the bake from the repository root:

   ```powershell
   unity command script eval --file tools/bake-unity-vfx.cs --project-path .cache/vfx-import --timeout 180
   python tools/build-external-vfx.py
   ```

The bake reads the game's camera elevation, uses fixed particle seeds, restores render settings,
rejects missing materials, empty renders and clipped captures, and closes its preview scene.
URP and Built-in effects use their respective render pipelines. Existing sprite-sheet grids are
read directly; additive-only textures have their transparent background reconstructed. Projectiles
are captured and centered independently of their movement through the live arena. The publisher converts
additive RGB to transparent PNGs, fits complete animations with uniform scaling and padding, and
retains the unmodified baked masters locally. Pillow and NumPy are required in the art environment.

Verification is being refreshed for the expanded recipes. Human visual approval remains pending.
