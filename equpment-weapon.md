# Weapon types

## Ranged (one-handed) - can dual-wield with off-hand

- one-handed sword
- one-handed axe
- dagger
- mace (can't dual-wield with off-hand)
- rod (can't dual-wield with off-hand)
- wand (can't dual-wield with off-hand)

## Two-handed

- Staff
- Spear
- Two-handed sword
- Two-handed axe

## Ranged (two-handed)

- Bow
- Crossbow

# Off-hand only

- Shield
- Book
