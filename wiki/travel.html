Travel and the Waypoint

The world is a hex sheet and a walk is a count of blocks. Blocks come out of the lattice coordinates, never a typed distance, so a route has a length and never a price.

One block

Walking mints no gold, so a long walk is a loot question and never a money question.

Every settlement, from the start

A warp is priced per block at the destination settlement’s own band (waypoint_warp.m_per_block against goldPerMinute), so a short hop is cheap and a crossing of the map is not. It buys back the walk’s time and nothing else.

The Waypoint

Every pair of the 18 settlements is walkable: there is no edge list and no route to buy (road.graph_rule). The sheet’s cells, pins and walk stretches are gated against the same lattice, so a distance on this page is the distance on the ground — see World.
