Crafting

Stones change what a piece is; two of them also move how high it sits — Ascend the band, Upgrade the Gear line. Use the settlement Craft bench to drag stones from your stack onto an equipped or carried target. Every price below is read, never typed.

Use the Craft bench

Gear crafting is a Settlement service. Camp cooking is the field exception; it does not craft gear.

  1. Drag a piece from Equipped or Inventory into the central bench slot.
  2. Hover a gear piece to inspect its full item detail and current Mod lines.
  3. Drag a stone from the stack onto the selected piece. Direct presses apply on drop; presses that need a line or Mod choice prepare the action for review and confirmation.
  4. Hover the gear again after the press to read its updated Mod lines.

The stones, one by one

Every Target cell reads the same way: which line (select = you name it · random = the stone draws it · all = every line in scope) · how many · what kind (unbound mod is the set past the Bound pair, bound mod the pair fixed at drop, frame mod line 1) · what happens, and a swap closes with what arrives. The frame mod belongs to no press except Corrupt, Polish and Reforge — those three are the only ones that touch it.

Quality ladder

Protection: 5 per piece from birth — each would-be break spends one and drops a level instead. Losing an Upgrade level also removes its Gear Mod points. A Repair stone revives one upgrade level below the pre-break level and refills protection. Broken gear has no combat effects and must be repaired before equipping. Online only.

How many lines a piece can gain

Remove refunds room — but every stone taken still counts toward the climbing fill price.

A piece remembers how many Unbound lines it dropped with, and Add stones may add 2 more in total — one line a press, never a bundle, and the fill price climbs with every press the piece has taken.
