Elements

Every Element, and no Element counters another: an Element names the status its hits leave and the one race that resists it. An Element declared ahead of its content reads a dash until its status and its skill rows land.

Where an Element is resisted

A race owns one Element (`mob.species[].element`) and answers it at a flat `mob.race_res_pct` in every zone it lives in. Every other Element lands on it unresisted — no table scales the hit, only the one flat line.
