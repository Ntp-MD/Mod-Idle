Combat

One incoming hit resolves in a fixed order — avoidance, then Armour, then Elemental resistance, then a pool. Every number is the engine at the reference build (every point spent evenly across the seven Core stats, level 190), so it is the line your own sheet is measured against, not a promise about it.

Elite and Boss

An Elite is a spawn flag forced onto a Large body, never a fifth size, so no two multipliers ever stack. A Boss is one named creature per zone (18 of them) and is the only place the Add, Replace and Corrupt stones are paid — see Away & bag. A mob spawns at your level clamped into its own zone’s range (mob.level_rule), which is why the Mob sheet prints a range.
