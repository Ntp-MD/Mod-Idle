Survival, potions and the herb farm

A fight you lose costs time, never a piece and never a level. These are the pools that buy the time back, and the two provisioning tracks that feed them.

What a Push costs

Nothing on this list is a number: a Push is the cost of the fight, not a tax on the character.

  • There is no death.rulea fight you lose rests you at camp and costs time only
  • A Push on the walk is the ordinary Push.rulecamp, rest, and back in on the block you were ambushed on — the walk is never cancelled and a block is never given back (road.push_rule)
  • A dungeon run forfeits its escrow.rulethe one place a Push costs loot — World
  • Your preset goes back to the main one.ruleand a cooldown already counting keeps counting (presets.cooldowns_persist_on_push)
  • A broken piece contributes nothing.rulethe Armourer clears it and refills protection — Crafting

Potions

Provisioning, not power: one shared cooldown of 30 sec, at most 3 a fight, switched off entirely on a Boss (potions.boss_suppressed), 2 weight each. Auto-use starts on at 30% Health and 25% Mana. That is why no potion moves a health line anywhere in the design.
