Provision (Herb · Farm · Potion · Shop)

import glossary.md import world.md import loot.md import crafting.md import item-list.md import combat.md import save.md import checks.md

Melvor-flavored provisioning without reopening the locked doors: Farming is the game's one life skill — a real leveled track (farm in engine.json) — but it grants no combat power, no potion healing on bosses (boss aura suppresses), and no gold↔stone exchange (the shop takes gold, crafting keeps the 7 stones · economy.md). The humanoid tribes drop a bottle of their own accord, at a chance derived from the herb stream itself (X49), so the mobs supplement the farm and never replace it. All flows are bounded and the loot funnel and the timeline never move (checks.md H1).

No food buffs. Provisioning is herbs → potions only; there is no food or cooked-meal buff layer. The timed-buff roster is already the buff layer (skill-pool-buff.md), so food would be a second one and the game is complex enough without it.

Herb

  • Drop: 2% per kill in mid zones, 3% in high zones, as a bundle of 1-3 zone-tier herbs (low / mid / high). Separate roll, small enough to leave F1-F3 intact. The rates live in engine.json herbs; the F13 row in checks.md is computed from them, so this line and that row cannot disagree.
  • Filter keeps herbs by default; herbs never dissolve into stones.
  • Misc category: herbs and potions stack inventory.stack_size.herb per slot and the character bag holds inventory.character_slots slots of stacks. The Herb pouch lines at the Porter buy one more slot each, not a bigger number, and engine.json inventory is the only place either figure is written.

Farm — the one life skill

Farming is Melvor's Farming discipline, copied and then trimmed to the farm part only (no fishing · mining · woodcutting): plant a seed on a plot, wait the growth timer, harvest for Farming XP plus herbs. It levels like any skill, and the level is what unlocks growing the higher herb tiers — but every output is a potion ingredient, never gear or power, so the whole track sits outside the mob_HP fold (AGENT.md §5). This block is generated by node tools/check.ts --write; the numbers live in engine.json farm.

Mid-tier unlock (L6) in 6.9 farm-days at base plots / 4.2 at full · high-tier unlock (L11) in 27.8 farm-days at base plots / 16.7 at full. Real-time gated on purpose — it is background provisioning, not a second combat ladder. <!-- END GENERATED:farm-model -->

  • Farm XP comes only from harvests, so the skill keeps pace with real time and never with combat: a player who fights more does not farm faster. The level is a provisioning ladder, not a power ladder — it gates which herb tier you can grow, and higher tiers only craft stronger potions (# Potion below), never stronger gear.

Potion (color ladder, weight, condensed — generated from engine.json potions)

The effect of every draught is a % of its pool, domiciled in engine.json potions and printed below — never hand-typed. Each tier adds a fixed step to a base (base + step × (tier − 1)); the mana line sits a step above the HP line because a mana pool is smaller than an HP pool at the same build.

  • Effect is % of the pool, derived as base + step × (tier − 1) from engine.json potions. Shared cooldown 30 sec · max 3 uses per fight · suppressed on bosses (combat.md §7 · bosses are won with casted heals).
  • Tiers follow herb tiers, so a higher Farming level and a higher zone grow stronger medicine, not cheaper medicine.
  • Weight counts toward weight_used (formula.md section 11): potion carriers pay the same Str tax as heavy armor. This replaces RO's zeny spam limit alongside the rules below.
  • Auto-use is configurable per line (HP line / Mana line): on/off toggle plus threshold slider (defaults HP < 30%, mana < 25%). Auto-use always drinks the best available tier first.
  • Uses are drawn from inventory and gated by the cooldown and per-fight cap in the block above. No cleanse potion exists: cleansing stays on Cleanse (skill-pool).
  • Threshold plus short normal-fight durations bound AFK abuse: potions almost never trigger in 1-sec farm kills — which is exactly why they are not modeled in tools/survival.ts and move no survival or timeline number (the 2026-10-03 survival re-run confirms SV1-SV6 hold).
  • Boss aura suppresses all potions. Bosses are won with casted heals only (combat.md section 7).

Shop (gold-priced)

  • Inventory is convenience only, never power: Plot deed 4, Plot deed 5 (Steward), Herb pouch II/III and stash tabs (Porter).
  • Prices in gold (economy.md): no convenience is priced in stones anymore, so the shop never competes with Reroll/Refine/Ascend for the same medium.
  • The shop never sells gear, Mods, potions, stones-for-stones at a profit, or anything that drops for free.
  • Who owns it and where — towns.md sections 4-5 split this single shop into NPC stalls across 6 capitals and 12 towns. Same rules, more owners.
