How to read a card

A card is one race inside one zone, so a race that lives in two zones prints a card in each. Nothing here is typed: the sheet is mobRoster(), mobEvasion(), mobResByElementOf(), mobStatsOf() and dropChance() printed out, so a wrong figure is a bug in the data or the engine, and node tools/verify.ts is what says which.

  • Kind — Normal, or an Elite flag forced onto a Large body (never a fifth size). A Boss is one named creature per zone and reads as a body class for a weapon's size_mult; its body sits in the Elite's bracket, so both print Large · alone.
  • Level — the spawn's own static level inside the zone's range (mob.species[].zone_levels); a spawn does not follow the attacker's level, so every figure is one number.
  • Core — the seven-stat block in that zone (mobStatsOf(speciesId, zoneId)), scaled from the anchor zone and always a whole number. Wis reads nothing on a mob and a physical body has no magic half, so those entries are 0.
  • Health — Base health (the published mob_HP curve × the body ÷ the zone body factor) and Base mana, then what the Core makes of them: Base HP + a flat hp-per-level grant + Vit × K_VIT_HP on the health line.
  • Offence — every line is Base + K_core through the one K table the player obeys: the damage tag (what Armour answers and what resistance answers), damage per second (mob.species[].power_base + the K term on the Str or Int its tag reads, over the zone mean, times the published mob_PS(L)), accuracy (Dex × K_DEX_ACC), Critical chance (Lck × K_LCK_CRIT) and Alignment (Dex × K_DEX_ALIGN).
  • Defence — Armour (Str × K_ARMOUR), Evasion (Core Dex × K_EVASION × the body's base percent) and dodge (Agi × K_MOB_DODGE).
  • Elemental resistance — each of the five Elements as a percent the player's hit of that Element is cut by. The race's own Element resists at a flat mob.race_res_pct in every zone; every other Element meets no res at all. The Cap the player obeys is the same Cap here.
  • XP a kill — xpPerKill(mob level), with the Elite and Boss multipliers already on the row.
  • Names (under the species) — the variant names that zone actually pays (mob.variants ∩ the sub-zone lists). A variant is a drop identity, not a stat line — the numbers are the species and the body.
  • Element — the one Element the race owns (mob.species[].element): fixed, the same in every zone, because a spawn rolls no Element of its own.
  • Drop — the gear roll and the herb roll at that band, the junk the named variants pay, which stream they lean, and the stones an Elite or a Boss adds.
