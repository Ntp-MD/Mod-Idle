How to read the sheet

Nothing here is typed: the sheet is mobRoster(), mobEvasion(), mobResByElementOf(), speciesPowerStatOf() and dropChance() printed out, so a wrong figure is a bug in the data or the engine, and node tools/verify.ts is what says which.

ColumnWhat it is
Racethe species row in mob.species. A weapon tag means its lineage may be the source of a weapon-slot piece; a humanoid tag adds the potion stream.
Namethe variant names that zone actually pays (mob.variants ∩ the sub-zone lists). A variant is a drop identity, not a stat line — the numbers are the species and the body.
Elementthe Elements this race can spawn with inside that zone: its own bias ∩ the zone list. The roll weighs the bias at ×mob.element_roll.bias_weight against any other Element the zone carries at ×mob.element_roll.other_weight.
Levelthe zone range. A mob spawns at the attacker's level clamped into that range, so the HP and XP columns are printed as a range too.
Sizethe body class and the group it comes in. An Elite is a flag on a Large body, never a fifth size; a Boss reads as a body class for a weapon's size_mult.
Core Statthe mob's fixed seven-stat block: the one flat base (mob.stat.base) through the species vector, with no level term — the same body at level 1 and at level 180, because only HP, PS and XP climb. Read through mobStatsOf().
Offensivewhat its hit is made of, and its damage per second — its own power (mob.species[].power_base + K_MOB_PS_STAT × the Str or Int its damage tag reads, over the zone mean) times the published mob_PS(L) — plus its accuracy rating, its crit chance and its Elemental Alignment, the number that decides how often its innate status lands.
Defensivehealth at both ends of the zone, Armour, Evasion (capped where the player is capped), its own dodge against a same-level attacker, and its per-Element resistance.
What it dropsthe gear roll and the herb roll at that band, the junk the named variants pay, which stream they lean, and the stones an Elite or a Boss adds.
XPwhat one kill pays at the two ends of the zone: xpPerKill(mob level), with the Elite and Boss multipliers already on the row.

Rendered from tools/data/*.json and engine/ — nothing here is typed by hand.
