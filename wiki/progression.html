Progression

Levels come from kills. Skill levels come from kills with that skill. Weapon Mastery comes from kills with that weapon. Nothing is priced by the clock.

Level steps

One row per level: the XP a level asks for is its own, read through the engine curve, never a second copy of it. The kill count is not an added rule — the curve is authored in kills (xp.kills_anchors) and a kill pays xpPerKill(mob level), so this is how many kills the level costs when you fight mobs at your own level. Fight a dearer zone and the count falls; fight a cheaper one and it rises. Past xp.plateau_from the bar holds flat while the kill count keeps falling, because a higher level pays the same bar against a mob at stat.mob_level_cap.

Weapon Mastery

Mastery never adds damage to a weapon type itself — that would make one type the right answer forever. It answers the weight question (Away & bag) and the drop question (Items), and it climbs at full rate while you are away.
