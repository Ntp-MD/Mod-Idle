Skills

Every skill is found as a drop and paid for with the same resource: mana. A skill levels up on kills with it in the list, up to 20 — and at that level a press deals 128.5% of its level-1 damage, a buff's or an aura's effect climbs to 180%, and every other effect reaches 128.5%.

The roster

Min and Max are the same list at the two ends of the skill-level ramp: a press reads its damage, everything else the lines it moves, and each line names itself. The damage figure is a press on the reference character, before crit. A row with a weapon beside its name fires only while that weapon is held; one without is open to its whole group.

What a status changes, and how far it climbs

Every figure is the status table the roll reads. A ROLLED status — an Element status, the bleed an axe’s line grants, or a stun — lands only when the Alignment gate lets it, and 20% of the Element hits that pass then carry their status. A row that declares its status outright is not a roll and never meets the gate.
